From field to loaf

Three slow steps, no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Grain we can name

    Rye and spelt from two farms in Małopolska. We buy the harvest, not the commodity price.

  2. 02

    Milled at dawn

    A granite stone turns slowly enough to keep the germ and the flavour. Flour is never older than the morning.

  3. 03

    Thirty-six hours

    Long, cool fermentation does the work a mixer cannot. We start Thursday's dough on Tuesday.
